72V 200A Premium BMS

High-Current BMS for Commercial Electric Vehicles

Engineered for commercial EVs and heavy-duty applications, the 72V 200A Premium BMS delivers industry-leading current handling with advanced thermal management, predictive analytics and OEM-grade reliability.

Key Features

200A Continuous

Predictive Analytics
